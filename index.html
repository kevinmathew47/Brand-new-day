The Camp Theme

Brand New Day

“Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone, the new is here!”

2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV)

What it means

The old has gone.
The new is here.

The poster says it in one picture: a young person standing in front of an open door, a cross above it — and everything that has been swinging at them held back on either side.

Fear. Doubt. Worry. Temptation. Past mistakes. Failure. Those blades are real, and most of us carry the marks. Brand New Day is three days to put them down, walk through the door, and find out what being a new creation actually means.

The New

The Message

Walk Through
The Door

Four sessions, four voices, one message carried from Friday evening to Sunday afternoon.

01

The Door Is Open

You don’t have to fight your way in. The way was opened at the Cross — the door is already standing wide, and the light is already spilling out.

02

The Chains Can Break

“I’m no longer a slave to fear.” Whatever had a hold on you this past year doesn’t get to define the next one.

03

Lighted To Lighten

Our Sakhyam motto isn’t decoration. You leave Thekkady carrying the flame — back into your school, your college, your home, your friends.

04

Where The War Was Won

The cross sits above the doorway for a reason. The victory isn’t something you earn on the way through — it’s what makes the way through possible.

05

You’re Not Alone

Trekking at sunrise, barbecue at night, campfire and talent night, a group photo on Sunday. This is the part you’ll still be talking about next year.

06

A New Creation Now

Not one day. Not someday. Now. Sunday closes with Holy Communion and a send-off devotion on the challenges waiting back home.

Camp Life

Three Days
At The Meadows

Know before you go

  • We leave the church at 5:00 PM on Friday, 14 August — be early, not on time.
  • Dinner is on the way; we reach Mar Thoma Meadows around 8:30 PM.
  • Saturday starts with a 7:00 AM trek, so lights go off at 10:00 PM sharp.
  • Flag hoisting on Saturday morning — it’s Independence Day.
  • Sunday closes with Holy Communion, a group photo, and lunch before we head home.

What to bring

  • Bible, notebook and a pen
  • Shoes you can actually trek in
  • A warm layer — Thekkady gets cold at night
  • Torch, water bottle, personal medicines
  • Toiletries and a towel
  • Something for talent night
  • Power bank — and the willingness to keep the phone in your bag
